# Presentation — AI-Powered Personalized Study Planner

## Slide 1 — Title
AI-Powered Personalized Study Planner
MCA Project

## Slide 2 — Problem
Students often use fixed timetables.
They may ignore weak topics, exam deadlines and different available study hours.

## Slide 3 — Objective
- Personalized scheduling
- Exam prioritization
- Weak-topic identification
- Progress tracking
- Analytics

## Slide 4 — Proposed Solution
Collect student data → calculate priority → allocate study sessions → track completion → analyze progress.

## Slide 5 — Key Features
- Dashboard
- Subject management
- Topic management
- Smart plan generation
- Study preferences
- Analytics
- Task completion

## Slide 6 — Personalization Algorithm
Priority depends on:
- Exam urgency
- Difficulty
- Importance
- Current progress

## Slide 7 — Technology
Python Flask
SQLite
HTML/CSS/JavaScript
Chart.js

## Slide 8 — System Architecture
User → Frontend → REST API → Planner Service → SQLite → Personalized Schedule

## Slide 9 — Demo
Show dashboard.
Add subject/topic.
Generate plan.
Complete task.
Show analytics.

## Slide 10 — Future Scope
- LLM integration
- Calendar integration
- Notifications
- Mobile application
- Adaptive learning

## Slide 11 — Advantages
- Saves planning time
- Focuses on weak areas
- Considers exam urgency
- Easy to update

## Slide 12 — Conclusion
The system demonstrates intelligent, personalized academic planning and provides a foundation for advanced AI-based learning assistants.
